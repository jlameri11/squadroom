For academies and clubs

  • Registration without the adminA public page families register on, with the questions you ask, the consents you need, kit sizes, sibling discounts, instalments and vouchers. PlayFootball first where the rules say so.
  • Squads, numbers and the rosterImport a spreadsheet or add players by hand. One jersey pool per season, so a U10 filling in for the U11s never clashes.
  • Chat that behavesTeam, age-group and club channels; announcements with "seen by"; replies that fold; voice notes; a message in each family's language. A child is never alone with an adult in a private chat.
  • Match day in your pocketFixtures from the league land in the right team's chat; RSVP in a tap; "can't play" with a reason; attendance in twenty seconds; a cancelled game pushes everyone, and cannot be muted.
  • Safeguarding built inWorking With Children Checks gate the roster; photo consent is checked before a photo is posted; concussions lock a child out of team sheets until a clearance is accepted.
  • Money, clearlyFees per program, who owes at a glance, a chase that goes out on its own, and a family that always sees one total.

For leagues

  • Run a whole seasonDivisions with their own rules, a draw checked before it goes out, home-and-away balance, a public match centre and ladders anyone can audit.
  • Team sheets, scores, disciplineSheets from both sides that lock before kick-off; scores that must agree; cards with the federation's codes; suspensions that keep a player off the sheet by name.
  • RefereesA pool, availability, appointments with a push, check-in by QR, reports, payment runs. Referees of the right grade can take open games themselves.
  • Clubs talk to clubsA change request between two managers, agreed inside a window the league sets, is done without a phone call. The league sees everything outstanding on one screen.

How the trial works

  1. 1Sign up with an emailA link, no password to remember. You are the owner of your club from the first minute.
  2. 2Set the club upCrest and colours, squads, the roster, a program, your coaches — a checklist that times itself. Most clubs are done inside half an hour.
  3. 3Bring the families inEach coach gets a link or a six-character code to say at training. A parent taps it, the coach approves, and they are in the team chat.
  4. 4Pay only when you decide toThe trial costs nothing. Pricing is published before anyone is asked for a cent, and it is always the club that pays.
