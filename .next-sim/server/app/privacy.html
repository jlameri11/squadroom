Squadroom

How we look after your info

Written for the kids who open a Squadroom page. The grown-up version is underneath.

If you're a player

  • You don't need an account. You open the page your coach or parent sent you, and you can see the draw, the scores and the ladder. That's it.
  • We don't watch you. The page has no ads and no trackers. We don't know who opened it.
  • Your name on the page is your club's choice, and your family's. Some clubs show full names, some show just “Leo W.”, some show no names. Your parent can switch your name off whenever they like, and that always wins.
  • Your photo is never on the internet. If your club keeps one, it's only on your player card, which only your family, your coaches and the referee on match day can open.
  • Your messages are with your team. A coach can't message you on your own — a parent is always in the chat too.
  • If something feels wrong, tell a grown-up. Your parent can report it from the app, and only the club's safeguarding people see it.

For parents and carers

What we hold about a child
Name, date of birth, squad and shirt number; the kit sizes, medical notes and emergency contacts you give the club; the agreements you sign; a photo if you add one. Nothing is collected from the child directly — children have no accounts.
Who can see it
The club's owners, admins and coaches. Medical notes: club staff only — never another club, never a league. The player card (photo, squad, number, date of birth): the family, the club's coaches, a league's officials while the child is on a season squad, and the opposing manager on match day only. Public competition pages show clubs and scores; names only by the club's rule, and never if you have switched them off.
What we never do
No advertising, no analytics on public pages, no sale or sharing of data, no address-book uploads, no fees charged to families by Squadroom. Children are never alone with an adult in a private chat.
Your controls
Settings → My family: each child's details, medical notes, agreements (agree or withdraw, versioned), kit sizes, the public-name switch, and who can see what. Settings → Your data: download everything we hold, or erase your account — the function tells you what is kept and why (safeguarding reports, the club's payment records, WWCC records, and the child's roster row, which belongs to the club).
Where it lives
In Sydney, Australia, permanently. Every club's data is separated from every other club's at the database level.
Questions
Ask your club first — they are the organisation that holds the record. This notice is reviewed against the Children's Online Privacy Code; a legal review is planned before Squadroom's second club.
