Squadroom

Interface prototype · v0

A working click-through of the six core screens, wired to the real permission model. Switch between Coach and Parent to see how the same channel changes: who can post, who sees the RSVP roster, who sees the names behind a reaction.

Try it

Role
Flip to Parent in the U8s channel — the composer becomes a reaction bar, the Admin tab disappears, and the poll stops showing who voted.
Mode
As Coach, use the posting-mode control in the channel header. It's channels.posting_mode, enforced in RLS, not just hidden here.
Brand
The colour swatches are academies.primary_colour — one deploy, every academy its own skin.

Phone size

The frame is a real device size, not a decoration — check the U8s channel at SE before you sign off on anything.

What each screen proves

Home
Upcoming before chatter. Next 7 days across all your teams with RSVP inline — a parent answers for both kids without opening anything.
Chat
Structure WhatsApp can't do. Pinned bar carries the next event and the latest announcement. Events and polls render as cards. Addresses become map links.
Event
Asymmetric visibility. Coaches get the attendance roster; parents see only their own answer.
Team
The only WhatsApp integration in v1 — tap-to-call and wa.me deep links, gated by the academy's directory setting.
Joining
Nobody signs up. A parent taps the coach's link in the WhatsApp group, types their name, and the coach approves. No password, no texted code, no SMS sent.
Admin
Migration in one paste. Bulk "Name, Phone" lines, live invite status, and a per-team QR that a coach drops into the old WhatsApp group.

Not built here

This is a static prototype — no Supabase, no auth, no realtime. It exists to settle layout, hierarchy and the permission rules before I write the UI against the schema. Data is illustrative: NRIFA, three teams, a September round.
